Summary of the Plot

A devastating demonic invasion has taken root in Redgrave City. As the terrifying Qliphoth tree drains the life from the streets, three unique devil hunters—Nero, Dante, and V—must unite to face an impossible threat.

Master over-the-top combat styles, wield explosive new weaponry, and awaken your ultimate power to take down a new demon king. The final battle for the Sparda legacy begins here.

Main Characters

Dante

Legendary Devil Hunter

The legendary son of Sparda battling deep within the Qliphoth roots to settle an old score and protect humanity.

Nero

Young Devil Hunter

Armed with his motorized sword Red Queen and high-tech prosthetic Devil Breakers, fighting to reclaim what was stolen from him.

Vergil

The Alpha & Omega

Dante's twin brother driven by an unrelenting pursuit of absolute power, wielding the dimensional blade Yamato.

Trish

Allied Demon Hunter

A formidable ally created in the image of Dante's mother, wielding thunder attacks and demonic martial arts.

Lady

Firearms Specialist

A seasoned human demon hunter providing heavy firepower and tactical backup with her signature rocket launcher.
