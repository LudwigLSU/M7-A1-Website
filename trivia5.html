Did You Know?

Release

Devil May Cry 5 launched on March 8, 2019 for PC, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One.

Three Heroes

It is the first game in the series where you switch between three playable characters: Nero, Dante and V.

RE Engine

Capcom built the game on the RE Engine, also used for Resident Evil 7 and the Resident Evil 2 remake.
