Summary of the Plot

Taking place on the isolated island of Vie de Marli, Devil May Cry 2 follows a stoic and seasoned Dante alongside Lucia, a guardian of the island's protector clan. Together, they are hired by Lucia's mother Matier—a warrior who once fought alongside Sparda—to retrieve sacred artifacts known as the Arcana.

Arius, the ruthless CEO of Uuroboros Corporation, seeks the Arcana to summon the powerful demon deity Argosax the Chaos and achieve godhood. As Dante and Lucia fight through city streets, ancient ruins, and corporate towers, Lucia uncovers dark secrets regarding her own origins while Dante prepares for a fateful duel against an apocalyptic demon king.

Main Characters

Dante

Protagonist / Devil Hunter

A legendary, taciturn demon hunter who relies on his trusted broadsword Rebellion, dual handguns Ebony & Ivory, and acrobatic prowess to eliminate threats.

Lucia

Co-Protagonist / Clan Guardian

A swift, dagger-wielding warrior from the Vie de Marli clan who fights to protect her homeland, unaware of her artificial demonic origins created by Arius.

Arius

Primary Antagonist / CEO

The power-hungry head of the Uuroboros Corporation who uses black magic and corporate wealth to attempt the ritual resurrection of Argosax.

Matier

Elder Guardian

Lucia's foster mother and an ancient guardian of Vie de Marli who once fought alongside Dante's father Sparda to seal away demonic forces.

Argosax

The Chaos / Demon King

An ancient demonic deity of immense power sealed away generations ago, threatening to plunge the human realm into eternal darkness if fully awakened.
