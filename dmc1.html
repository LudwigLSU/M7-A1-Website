Summary of the Plot

Devil May Cry follows Dante, a half-human, half-demon private investigator and demon hunter running the shop "Devil May Cry." His routine is shattered when a mysterious woman named Trish ambushes his office and reveals that Mundus, the Prince of Darkness whom Dante's father Sparda sealed away 2,000 years ago, is breaking out on Mallet Island.

Venturing into the gothic castle of Mallet Island, Dante battles demonic legions, confronts the honorable corrupted dark knight Nelo Angelo, and uncovers the truth about his twin brother and family heritage before facing the dark god Mundus.

Main Characters

Dante

The Son of Sparda

A witty, fearless half-demon mercenary seeking vengeance for the murder of his mother and brother.

Trish

The Mysterious Catalyst

A demon created by Mundus who bears an identical likeness to Dante's deceased mother, Eva.

Nelo Angelo

The Black Knight

An enigmatic armored warrior bound to Mundus's will who shares Dante's demonic swordsmanship.

Mundus

Prince of Darkness

The sovereign ruler of the underworld seeking to conquer the human world after centuries of imprisonment.

Griffon

Demon General

A colossal, thunder-wielding demon beast who tests Dante's strength in multiple confrontations across the island.
