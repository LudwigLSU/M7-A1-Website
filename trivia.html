Saga Trivia & Lore

01

Tony Redgrave Alias

Before events on Mallet Island, Dante used the alias "Tony Redgrave" as a mercenary and bounty hunter in the human world—a direct nod to the original prototype novel of the first game.

02

Strawberry Sundae Obsession

Dante's absolute favorite food is strawberry sundaes with extra toppings, a recurring running gag highlighting his relaxed human side despite being a powerful half-demon.

03

Resident Evil Origins

The original concept for Devil May Cry was born during the development of Resident Evil 4. Director Hideki Kamiya noticed the action felt too stylized and stylish for survival horror, prompting Capcom to turn it into its own independent IP.

04

The Legend of Sparda

Sparda, the legendary dark knight and father to Dante and Vergil, rebelled against the underworld single-handedly 2,000 years prior to save humanity, sealing the Hell Gate shut.

05

Yamato and Rebellion

The twins' signature swords carry deep symbolism: Dante's sword Rebellion represents his awakening human heart and power, while Vergil's katana Yamato represents his unyielding demonic heritage.

06

Royal Guard Mastery

Among all of Dante's combat styles introduced in DMC 3, the Royal Guard style requires the most precise timing, letting skilled players absorb enemy attacks and unleash devastating counter-damage.
