Quick facts about Devil May Cry 3: Dante's Awakening, then test what you know.

Did You Know?

Chronological First

Devil May Cry 3 is a prequel set years before the original game, showing a young Dante opening his devil-hunting shop.

Combat Styles

It introduced the iconic Style system—Trickster, Swordmaster, Gunslinger, and Royalguard—defining the series' high-speed combat.

Special Edition

Due to the original North American release's brutal difficulty, Capcom released a Special Edition in 2006 featuring Vergil as a playable character.
