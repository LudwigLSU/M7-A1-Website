Summary of the Plot

Set as a prequel to the original game, Devil May Cry 3 follows a younger, brasher Dante as he is challenged by his estranged twin brother, Vergil. Vergil aims to unseal the barrier connecting the human and demon realms using the massive tower known as Temen-ni-gru and their father Sparda's amulet.

Joined by Mary (who adopts the name "Lady"), a vengeful demon hunter seeking to slay her corrupt father Arkham, Dante must scale the deadly tower, conquer demonic forces, unlock his true Devil Trigger potential, and prevent ultimate chaos from consuming the earth.

Main Characters

Dante

Protagonist / Devil Hunter

A cocky half-demon mercenary seeking revenge for his mother's murder, armed with his broadsword Rebellion and dual pistols Ebony & Ivory.

Vergil

Primary Antagonist

Dante's stoic twin brother who embraces his demonic heritage. Wielding the Yamato, Vergil stops at nothing to attain his father's demon power.

Lady (Mary)

Demon Hunter

A tough human demon hunter wielding the Kalina Ann rocket launcher. She enters the tower to exact vengeance upon her corrupted father.

Arkham

Mastermind

A scholar obsessed with demonic power who manipulates both Dante and Vergil into opening the portal to the demon world for his own gain.

Jester

Enigmatic Fool

A bizarre, clown-like figure who taunts and guides Dante throughout the Temen-ni-gru tower, hiding sinister secret motives.
