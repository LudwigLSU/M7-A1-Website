Plot

Set on the isolated Dumary Island, the story follows Dante and Lucia as they attempt to stop Arius, the CEO of the global megacorporation Uroboros. Arius seeks to gather four sacred artifacts called the Arcana to break the seal between the human realm and the Netherworld, allowing him to absorb the power of the ancient demon king Argosax the Chaos and gain immortality.

During her journey, Lucia makes the agonizing discovery that she is not human, but an artificial "Arachne" demon created by Arius who was deemed defective, abandoned, and later rescued and raised by her adoptive mother, Matier. Despite questioning her own humanity, Lucia continues fighting to protect the island.

Though Dante and Lucia manage to interrupt the ritual, the gateway to the Demon Realm begins to open anyway. Dante leaves Lucia in the human world to deal with a mutated Arius while he rides his motorcycle directly into the portal to eliminate Argosax. Dante successfully defeats Argosax in the Demon World and drives deeper into Hell, leaving his ultimate fate in this chapter open-ended.
