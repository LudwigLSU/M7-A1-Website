Summary of the Plot

In the gothic castle city of Fortuna, a religious organization known as the Order of the Sword worships the Dark Knight Sparda as a deity. During a holy ceremony, the peace is shattered when the legendary demon hunter Dante bursts through the ceiling and executes the Order's spiritual leader, Sanctus.

A young holy knight named Nero engages Dante in combat before being tasked to hunt him down. As Nero pursues Dante, he uncovers the Order's dark experiments with demonic power and discovers Sanctus's true plan: to activate a colossal artificial deity known as "The Savior" using Sparda's bloodline and the Yamato blade.

Main Characters

Nero

Holy Knight / Protagonist

A rebellious young knight of the Order possessing a demonic right arm called the Devil Bringer, armed with the motorized broadsword Red Queen.

Dante

Legendary Devil Hunter

The seasoned son of Sparda who intervenes in Fortuna to investigate the Order's illicit weaponization of the demonic realm and recover the Yamato.

Kyrie

Songstress of Fortuna

Nero's childhood companion and adoptive sister whose gentle nature serves as Nero's primary motivation to fight and protect his loved ones.

Credo

Supreme General

Kyrie's older brother and supreme commander of the Holy Knights. A disciplined swordsman loyal to the Order until he discovers its corruption.

Sanctus

Grand Master / Mastermind

The megalomaniacal spiritual head of the Order of the Sword who seeks to conquer the world by fusing himself with the gigantic god-machine, The Savior.
